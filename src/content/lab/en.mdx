## Alpine Risk & Exposure Modeling

In high-altitude mountaineering, safety is not merely a subjective feeling; it is a probability function of environmental exposure, physical exhaustion, and routing decisions. We model the **Exposure Index ($EI$)** of a planned route mathematically:

$$
EI = \sum_{k=1}^{n} (D_k \cdot \alpha_k \cdot \mu_k)
$$

Where:
*   $D_k$ is the distance traversed in segment $k$ (in kilometers).
*   $\alpha_k$ is the average steepness or incline coefficient of segment $k$.
*   $\mu_k$ is the meteorological/terrain variability factor (determined by snow pack, wind speed, and precipitation levels).

By keeping $EI$ below a calculated critical safety threshold, we can make objective, quantitative go/no-go decisions in volatile alpine conditions.
