## Philosophy and Architecture of Thought

My professional and academic work is guided by a constant search for logical formalization and the minimization of systemic noise. I believe that software engineering of excellence shares the same rigorous foundations of abstract algebraic structures.

For example, when modeling data flows or designing fault-tolerant systems, we often face the need to measure the uncertainty and dispersion of information in communication channels. This concept is formalized through **Shannon Entropy** $H(X)$:

$$
H(X) = -\sum_{i=1}^{n} P(x_i) \log_2 P(x_i)
$$

Where $P(x_i)$ represents the probability of occurrence of a given state of the system. The balance of this flow is the boundary between resilience and structural collapse.
